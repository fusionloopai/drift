What you just did — the steps

  1. Named one goal, in the present tense, as if it already happened.
  2. Broke it into checkpoints you can actually check.
  3. Put a real date on every one of them.
  4. Named the obstacles before they showed up, and what you'd do about each.
  5. Listed what you'd need to learn, buy, or build.
  6. Named the people whose help you'll need.
  7. Reordered it until the sequence was true.
The last move is the one this game can't do for you: take the nearest checkpoint and break it down to daily actions. A goal you touch every day stops being a goal and starts being who you are.

Next: The Dream Designer Blueprint

This game gave you one goal, fully built. The Blueprint looks at the machinery underneath it — the reason most goals quietly die isn't the plan, it's the operating system running it.

It's a short assessment. You answer honestly across five areas, and it scores you out of 100:

  • Goals — do you write them down, picture them, say them out loud, look at them again?
  • Mindset & belief — do you actually believe you'll pull it off, and do you go again when you don't?
  • Daily practice — is there a morning you keep, and a block of time the important work is safe in?
  • Environment & influence — do the people and inputs around you pull you forward or hold you level?
  • Action & follow-through — do you finish, do you track it, and how fast do you get back on?

Your score puts you in one of four tiers, and each one gets its own guidance:

The DrifterLife happens to you more than through you. Not a character flaw — an unmade decision.
The DreamerYou know what you want. It lives in your head, the one place a goal can't work from.
The BuilderYou set goals and return to them. What's missing is rhythm — the daily contact.
The DesignerYou're running the loop most people never start. Your work now is depth, not repair.

It takes a few minutes, and it tells you the one part of the loop that's actually breaking — so the next goal you set doesn't need this much scaffolding to survive.
